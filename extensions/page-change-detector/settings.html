Monitors are created and managed in the Page Monitors panel, opened from the bell button in the toolbar. Each monitor loads its page on a schedule, compares the text (or just the elements you picked), and alerts you when something changed.
The chat agent can list, create, check, pause, and delete monitors through the page_monitor tools. Creating or deleting a monitor from chat asks for approval first.
