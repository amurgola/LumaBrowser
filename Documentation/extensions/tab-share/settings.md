# settings.html (tab-share)

`extensions/tab-share/settings.html`

The Tab Share settings page fragment the shell mounts for
`manifest.settings.htmlFile`: how it works, the availability callout
(`#ext-ts-availability`), the shared-tab list (`#ext-ts-list`, `#ext-ts-stopAll`),
the streaming controls (`#ext-ts-rtcEnabled`, `#ext-ts-turnEnabled`,
`#ext-ts-turnPort`, `#ext-ts-turnHost` and their status lines) and "Good to
know". [renderer.js](renderer.md) binds and fills it.
