# PaintedBeatPrompt

`extensions/roleplay-mode/prompts/PaintedBeatPrompt.js`

The base-model plate prompt for a painted (physical contact) beat.

## Methods

- `PaintedBeatPrompt.build(data, animaPrompt, directorShot, momentEntries)` a
  solo contact beat becomes a face-only reaction close-up without the literal
  action; otherwise the action, kiss tags, props and close framing are appended.
- `PaintedBeatPrompt.SOLO_NEGATIVE`.
