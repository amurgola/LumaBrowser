# FigurePrompts

`extensions/roleplay-mode/prompts/FigurePrompts.js`

Edit-model prompts for a character's full-body figures.

## Methods

- `FigurePrompts.fromHead(char)` invent the whole body from a head portrait.
- `FigurePrompts.moment(char, poseClause, bodyCue)` the posed figure on the
  chroma plate, the backdrop anchored at both ends.
- `FigurePrompts.MOMENT_NEGATIVE`.
